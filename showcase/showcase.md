Cascade is Klondike solitaire played with a Draw Three stock. A fresh shuffled deck opens across seven columns, with one face-up card at the foot of each column.

Build each column downward in alternating colors, uncovering the cards beneath. Only a King can fill an empty column. Move Aces and their suits upward to the four foundations, building each from Ace to King.

Turn the stock to reveal groups of three cards on the waste. Play its top card onto a column or a foundation; when the stock runs out, turn the waste back over and play through it again.

Drag a card or a valid run to move it, and double-click a playable card to send it home. Finish all four foundations and watch the deck take flight across the felt.
